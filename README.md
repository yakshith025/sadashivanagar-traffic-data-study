# Traffic Data Collection and Analysis: Sadashivanagar PS Junction

A field study of a three-leg signalized junction in Bengaluru: counting, timing and measuring traffic by hand to diagnose why the signal performs poorly, then proposing new signal timing plans.

## Data collected

- Classified **turning-movement counts** and vehicle composition
- **Queue lengths** per approach
- **Spot speeds** over a 30 m trap
- **Signal phase timings**

## Key findings

- Median spot speed was about **8.5 m/s**, and 89% of vehicles travelled below 11 m/s (WB approach, evening peak).
- **Signal efficiency ranged from 30% to 75%**, below the 70–85% target for urban intersections. The SB-West and WB-North movements were below 32%.
- Causes: unbalanced phase splits, start-up lost time from heavy vehicles, and lane-changing that reduces effective capacity.
- **Recommendations:** separate **peak (~150 s) and off-peak (~100 s) signal timing plans**, plus channelization and pedestrian safety measures.

The data from this study was later used to build a VISSIM microsimulation model of the junction.
